11.2 Rectilinear Motion
- 先固定座標系統
- $\overrightarrow{F}=m×\overrightarrow{a}$   , in 牛頓座標系統
	- average velocity, $\frac{dx}{dt}$
		t  → 0
		v = $\frac{dx}{dt}$, instantaneous velocity
	- average velocity, $\frac{dv}{dt}$
			t  → 0
			a = $\frac{dv}{dt}$, instantaneous velocity
- $\overrightarrow{v}$, $\overrightarrow{a}$ are vectors 
- x(t) = position vector
	- $\overrightarrow{v}(t)=\frac{dx(t)}{dt}$
	- $\overrightarrow{a}(t)=\frac{dx(v)}{dt}=\frac{dx}{dx}*\frac{dx}{dt}=v*\frac{dv}{dx}$
